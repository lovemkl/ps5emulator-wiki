## CPU and RAM: The Underrated Half

PS5 emulation is not a GPU-only bill. The console's eight-core custom CPU means translation-heavy, multi-threaded work — the practical requirement is a modern six-core-plus CPU with strong per-core performance, and the community's demonstrated systems cluster on current-generation mid-range and better processors. RAM demands run similarly modern: 16GB is the working floor with 32GB the comfortable zone, because emulation overhead rides on top of the game's own footprint. A PS5 Emulator for PC on a strong GPU with an aging CPU bottleneck is one of the scene's most common disappointments — the requirements are a system, not a card.
