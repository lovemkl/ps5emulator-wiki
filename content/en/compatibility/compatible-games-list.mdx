## How to Read This List

Every entry carries a status, and the statuses mean specific things in emulation-scene vocabulary. **Playable**: runs from start to finish at framerates a normal person accepts, with documented caveats at most. **Runs**: substantially playable with real compromises — notable glitches, audio issues or settings-bound performance. **Boots**: starts and reaches in-game content, but not yet a way to play through. The statuses move in one direction over time — boots become runs, runs become playable — and the speed of that movement across the whole list is the scene's headline statistic.

Two honesty rules govern this page. First, statuses are attached to demonstrated, community-replicated results — not claims from release notes alone. Second, hardware context is mandatory: a result without the hardware that produced it is a rumor. When we say a game runs, we mean it runs on named, ordinary hardware you can buy.
