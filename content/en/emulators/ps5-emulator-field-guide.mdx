## Why This Generation Is Different

PS3 and Switch emulation matured because the hardware gap was generous and the architecture understood. The PS5 generation inverts both: the hardware is a near-modern PC (which helps translation tools like AnyPS5) but the software stack — the system libraries, the shader pipeline, the security model — is deep and undocumented. That is why the honest field is small: three credible projects, each betting on a different translation strategy, rather than a dozen forks of one mature core.

Practical expectations: check each project's compatibility list before expecting anything from a specific game, buy hardware headroom (the AnyPS5 route checks your CPU's instruction set at startup for a reason), and treat "PS5 emulator that runs everything" claims as the scam tell they are.

## The Fake Emulator Playbook

Every wave of emulation interest spawns the same scams, and "PS5 emulator" searches are their prime hunting ground. The tells, unchanged for a decade: a polished site promising full compatibility before any real project has it, a downloader/installer instead of public source code, activation keys or surveys, and no public development history. The verification rule is absolute: **a real emulation project has a public repository and release notes; everything else is marketing**. The fakes do not merely fail — they carry malware, and they charge for what the real projects give away free.

If a site asks for money, a key, or a survey before you can "unlock" a PS5 emulator, close the tab. Then come back and read the real ones: [Kyty](/emulators/kyty-ps5), [AnyPS5](/emulators/anyps5-github), and the [field comparison](/emulators/anyps5-and-sharpemu).
